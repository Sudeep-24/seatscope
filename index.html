ABOUT SEATSCOPE

Making Counselling Research Simpler

SeatScope is designed to help students understand their college and course possibilities using historical counselling data.

Instead of manually comparing multiple cutoff records, students can enter their preferences and explore relevant options in one place.

SeatScope is a decision-support tool. It does not replace official counselling information or guarantee admission.

Important Information

SeatScope provides historical indicators based on available cutoff records. Historical closing ranks may change from year to year and from round to round. The results should be used for research and choice-list planning only and are not admission guarantees.
